Bitcoiners are everywhere. BitcoinWalk should be too.

Walkie Talkie is the open-source app for turning that idea into real local communities. Find a BitcoinWalk in your city, meet Bitcoiners face to face, and keep the community connected on Nostr—without asking permission from a centralized platform.

A global movement, rooted locally
Browse the worldwide BitcoinWalk city mosaic before signing in. Choose your city and it becomes your home screen, with its public page and upcoming walk details always close at hand. If BitcoinWalk has not reached your city yet, start one and put it on the map.

From city streets to Nostr
Every BitcoinWalk can have its own NIP-29 community conversation. City and BitcoinWalk Global chats are publicly readable; sign in with Nostr to join and speak. Verified organizers also have a private Hosts space for coordinating the movement.

Your identity, your keys
No phone number. No email account. Create a Nostr identity, import your key, connect through Amber, or use a remote signer. Your identity can move with you instead of being trapped inside the app.

Talk directly
Send encrypted direct messages, media and files. Reply to specific messages, react quickly, mention people and control notifications room by room.

Built in the open
Walkie Talkie is BitcoinWalk infrastructure: open source, built on open protocols and released under the MIT License. Bluetooth mesh is visible as a Coming soon preview while resilient offline communication is developed and tested.

Before you start
- Back up keys created in the app before relying on them.
- Nostr content is distributed through independent relays. Availability and moderation can vary by relay.
- Background delivery is best-effort and may be limited by Android battery settings.
