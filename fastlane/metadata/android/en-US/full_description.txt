Walkie Talkie is BitcoinWalk's open-source companion for discovering walks and staying connected with local communities.

Choose your city
Browse the BitcoinWalk city mosaic before signing in. Your chosen city becomes your home screen, with its public page and upcoming walk details always close at hand.

Join the conversation
Sign in with a Nostr identity to request access to the selected city group and BitcoinWalk Global. City and Global conversations can be read publicly; posting requires membership. The private Hosts group is shown only to verified organizers.

Use your own Nostr identity
Create a new identity, import a key, connect through Amber, or use a remote signer. Your account is based on Nostr rather than a phone number or email address.

Message privately
Send encrypted direct messages, media, files, replies and reactions. Guide notices keep important BitcoinWalk account updates together in the app.

Stay close
Compatible devices can exchange nearby messages over Bluetooth Low Energy when internet access is limited. Availability depends on the device and operating system.

Before you start
- Back up keys created in the app before relying on them.
- Nostr content is distributed through independent relays. Availability and moderation can vary by relay.
- Background delivery is best-effort and may be limited by Android battery settings.
- Nearby messaging requires Bluetooth permissions and compatible hardware.

Walkie Talkie is released under the MIT License. Source code, protocol documentation and security information are available in the public repository.
